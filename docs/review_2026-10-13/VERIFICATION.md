## Independent source and artifact interpretation

The new source sample was deliberately selected after evaluation to cover 2018–2021, high/modest/low service and trades. It is a transcription audit, not random population validation or exploratory model selection. All 30 cached JSON digests were independently checked. The final reviewer separately parsed raw ESPN responses, confirmed 58 player/seasons and exact season-set agreement for all 12 NBA participants, and recomputed all 174 fields without calling the new audit parser. Games/starts matched exactly; minute comparisons also satisfy the stricter half-rounding bound 0.05 × games. ESPN does not independently provide VORP in these responses.

The new parser initially failed because date-prefixed total rows were summed with team stints. Fourteen apparent disagreements were caused by this audit-tool bug; initial summaries/comparisons/log remain. The correction recognizes explicit total labels and rejects ambiguous duplicates. The full cached replay and dedicated regression tests then passed. No frozen provider data was changed.

Independently inspected all 130 saved bundles (162 member estimators): frozen schemas/hyperparameters, correct maturity-fitted numeric medians/missingness indicator columns, and constant means/prevalences match. Stored SHAP encoded data exactly matches current training-fitted preprocessing, and additive contributions reconstruct the forest's asinh-scale output within 2.67e-15. These checks do not validate causal explanations or every provider field.
