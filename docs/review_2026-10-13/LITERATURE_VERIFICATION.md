This is a verification of specific references and claims, not a claim of exhaustive discovery of every prior NBA forecasting study. Retrieval failures are distinguished from nonexistent citations. No citation or bibliographic field was invented.

## Data/provider references and access limits

The [Gonzaga repository](https://github.com/mathgonzaga/NBADraftModel) identifies Matheus Gonzaga as author and Bart Torvik as college-data origin. Its own README acknowledges that a chronological split still uses career NBA statistics through 2020-2021. The [secondary archive](https://github.com/warrensuca/nba-role-player-prediction-model) documents outcome-based role-player filters and best-three-season labels in its separate modeling project. The active study appropriately treats these as raw archives and does not claim to inherit their fitted models, performance-filtered population or labels.

Both live repository landing pages were retrieved. The exact pinned-commit tree pages returned cache misses in the web tool; remote commit contents were not independently fetched in this literature review. Local snapshot/manifest checks and collection-script verification remain separate computational tasks.

The [Basketball Reference BPM methodology](https://www.basketball-reference.com/about/bpm2.html?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block) explicitly dates version 2.0 to February 2020, describes the original 2014 release and defines VORP from BPM and exposure. This prompted the new distinction between sporting follow-up maturity and historical metric-definition availability. The archived source's exact VORP vintage remains unresolved. The [NBA combine landing page](https://www.nba.com/stats/draft/combine) exists and was retrieved; this alone does not validate the numerical cached combine records. Direct [Torvik statistics](https://barttorvik.com/playerstat.php) retrieval returned 403; [ESPN historical draft landing](https://www.espn.com/nba/draft/history) was inaccessible in the web tool. No independent live numerical verification was claimed for those failures.

A direct Python HTTPS fetch for the Sloan landing page was blocked by the workspace network restriction (WinError 10013). The available web tool provided the primary landing/indexed evidence above, so no network escalation was needed and no failed retrieval was described as a successful full-text download.

## Related work considered without adding manuscript claims

[Ranisavljev et al. (2021)](https://rua.ua.es/dspace/bitstream/10045/106147/6/JHSE_16-3_02.pdf), Journal of Human Sport and Exercise 16(3), 493-502, DOI 10.14198/jhse.2021.163.02, studies combine measures and rookie performance in 58 appearance-filtered players. [Isaiah Hartman's SAS conference paper (2019)](https://support.sas.com/resources/papers/proceedings19/3947-2019.pdf), NBA Draft Analytics: Boom or Bust?, constructs a rookie performance index and compares combine-based regression/trees with a stratified random split. These further establish combine-to-rookie analysis as prior work but were not needed to support additional manuscript claims or new model choices.

## Verification conclusion

No fabricated citation was found among the investigated research references. The implemented corrections are the missing directly relevant 2024 chronological forecasting comparison, Li-Cao's pagination, Wiethuechter's prose spelling, and source/version chronology qualifications. Source accuracy and historical availability remain separate unresolved questions. Full paper access and replication were not available for every cited study, and verification of metadata/abstracts must not be described as independent reproduction of their findings.
